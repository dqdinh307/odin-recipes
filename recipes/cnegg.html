Description of the dish

Chinese tomato egg stir-fry is fast, easy and cheap

The tangy tomatoes, mixed with rich scrambled eggs, a hint of sugar will surprise you at how tasty it is.

Ingredients of the dish

  • 4 small tomatoes
  • 1 scallion
  • 4 eggs
  • Vegetable oil
  • Sugar

Steps needed to complete the dish

  1. Cut tomatoes into small edges
  2. Crack 4 eggs into a bow and season with salt and pepper
  3. Preheat the work over medium heat then scramble the eggs
  4. Stir fry for 2 minutes and mix everything together then cover the wok
  5. Uncover then continue to stir fry over high heat to your likingsssss
